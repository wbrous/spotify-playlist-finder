Spotify Playlist Finder

Finds playlists whose name matches exactly, case-sensitive. Spotify's own search fuzzy-matches; this filters that noise out. Searches every page Spotify allows (offset up to its hard 1000 limit).

Upload or paste a cover-art screenshot, crop to just the artwork, add a few search keywords, and results are ranked by visual similarity (perceptual hash) to your crop — best matches first, nothing excluded.

Drag to select the crop region.
